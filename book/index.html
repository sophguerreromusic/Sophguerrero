About

Sophia Guerrero is a House and Tech House DJ and producer from Manila, Philippines. Her catalogue spans Toolroom Records, Milk & Sugar, There Was Jack, HouseU, Alleanza and Wh0 Worx, with support from Chris Lake, Jamie Jones, Claptone, Shiba San, Dennis Cruz, Nic Fanciulli, Francis Mercier, Blond:ish, Roger Sanchez, Sam Divine, Boys Noize, Don Diablo, Martin Ikin, TSHA and Mark Knight.

Her music has been played on BBC Radio 1 by Arielle Free and featured on Danny Howard's Club Mix. Behind the decks she's a fixture of Manila's club scene — precise, hypnotic, built for peak-time dancefloors.
