Info.

Sophia Guerrero is a House and Tech House DJ and producer from Manila, Philippines. Her catalogue spans Toolroom Records, Milk & Sugar, There Was Jack, HouseU, Alleanza and Wh0 Worx, with support from TSHA, Nicole Moudaber, Mark Knight, Roger Sanchez and Fedde Le Grand.

Her music has been played on BBC Radio 1 by Arielle Free and featured on Danny Howard's Club Mix. Behind the decks she's a fixture of Manila's club scene — precise, hypnotic, built for peak-time dancefloors.
